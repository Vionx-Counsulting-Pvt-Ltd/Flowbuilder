---
name: Staff Onboarding register policy
description: Staff creation, editing authority and deferred Evaluation scope.
---

Staff Onboarding needs Dashboard, Active Staff, Evaluation and Inactive Staff views. Final onboarding approval automatically creates an active employee record; older approved requests also qualify.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose automatic staff creation after final approval. They selected employee probation/evaluation for Evaluation but said its design must happen later.

**How to apply:** Keep Evaluation unconfigured until the owner defines that process. Do not invent probation dates, ratings, evaluation approvals or status transitions.

Employee information and employment status are maintained separately from the approved request. Admins and explicitly designated staff managers may edit both; admin designation is not general workflow-builder or request-approval authority.

**Why:** The owner requested staff management by admins or specific people, while the existing request policy only permits correction of returned requests.

**How to apply:** Never reopen or rewrite approved onboarding history to update employment details. Staff editors update independent values, not onboarding formulas; protect stale writes. Show ordinary members only directory summaries, not full onboarding answers or attachments.
